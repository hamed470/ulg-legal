ULG Bot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, ULG Bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames, command interactions, and configuration or moderation records.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the bot, provide its features, maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.

3. Data Storage

Some information may be stored in databases or server logs for the operation of the bot. Access is limited to authorized personnel.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, or protect the service.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Users may contact the ULG Development team to request information about applicable deletion options.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when bot features or data practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the ULG Development team through the official ULG Discord community.

9. Message Content and Presence

Message content is processed only to generate in-Discord logs and ticket transcripts. Transcripts are uploaded to Discord only and are not stored on any external server. Presence data is read live only to check whether a staff member is online and is never stored. Data is never sold or shared with third parties.

10. Contact

Support server: https://discord.gg/ulg
