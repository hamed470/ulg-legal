ULG Bots - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Who We Are

This policy applies to the Discord applications operated by the ULG community: "إدارة الوظائف" (ULG Management Bot) and "ULG Tickets". Both applications run only inside the Discord servers where they are installed, and this policy explains what data they process, why, and how users can request its deletion.

2. Information We Collect

  • Discord user IDs, usernames, server nicknames and avatars.
  • Staff codes, roles and department membership managed by server administrators.
  • Attendance records: clock-in / clock-out timestamps and calculated hours.
  • Report counts, staff points, penalties, warnings and blacklist entries.
  • Ticket metadata: ticket owner, channel, type, status, claimer and timestamps.
  • Configuration and moderation records created by server administrators.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Passwords, account credentials, tokens or payment information.
  • Email addresses, phone numbers or physical addresses.
  • Direct messages that are not related to a support ticket.
  • Browsing activity, advertising identifiers or data from other applications.

4. How We Use Information

Data is used exclusively to operate the bots inside the Discord servers where they are installed: attendance and operation panels, staff management, job applications, ticket handling, moderation logs and support replies. We never sell, rent, license or share data with third parties, and we never use it for advertising.

5. Message Content

Message content is processed only to generate in-Discord moderation logs and ticket transcripts, and to run prefix commands typed by users. Ticket transcripts are generated in memory and uploaded to Discord only (the ticket owner's direct messages and our logs channel). Message content is not stored on any external server, is never sold or shared, and is never used to train machine learning or AI models.

Because reading messages is required for these features to work, users cannot opt out of message processing while using them. Server administrators can disable logging features at any time, and any user may contact us to request deletion of the records generated from their messages.

6. Presence Data

Our applications do not request or store Discord presence (online status) data. If a future feature needs it, presence would be read live only and never stored, logged or shared.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is not shared with third parties except when strictly necessary to operate the service (for example, our own hosting and database providers acting on our behalf), to comply with legal obligations, or to protect the service and its users from abuse.

8. Storage, Security and Retention

Data is stored in our own MySQL database on a private server. Access is restricted to authorized ULG Development personnel, and all traffic between Discord and our bots is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS).

Records are retained only for as long as they are needed for attendance, staff points and ticket history. Data is deleted when it is no longer necessary, when a member leaves the relevant staff system, or when a deletion request is received. Log messages live inside Discord channels controlled by the server administrators.

9. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us in our support server: https://discord.gg/ulg. Deletion requests are handled within 7 days.

10. Children

Our applications are not directed to anyone under the age of 13, or under the minimum age required by the laws of their country. We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when bot features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.
